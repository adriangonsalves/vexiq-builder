Wrap chain round two sprockets.

1. Pick the sprockets with **Tools → Chain builder** [[Tools]] (1).
   ![The Tools menu open with Chain builder marked](shot:chain-tools-menu)
2. {{Double-click the sprocket | Tap **Join**, then the sprocket}} (2) and press `W`.
   ![Sprocket picked](shot:chain-sprocket)
3. Use [[Tools → Explode]] and read [Gears](help:gears#meshing) or [Quick](help:quick-start).

> **Tip** Keep sprockets in one plane.

> **Watch out** The chain sags. Fix it.

<!-- more -->

## If the chain sags
Look at the mark (1) first.

![The slack run](shot:chain-sag)

Then move a sprocket. Open [the words page](help:words).
