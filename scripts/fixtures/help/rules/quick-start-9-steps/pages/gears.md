Gears turn together when their teeth meet.

1. Pick a gear with **Tools** [[Tools]] (1) and a second gear (2).
   ![Two gears with marks](shot:gear-pair)
2. {{Click the gear | Tap the gear}} to turn it.

> **Tip** Keep gears one pitch apart.

See [the bar](help:context-bar#every-button-on-the-bar) and [start](help:quick-start).

<!-- more -->

## If gears slip
Move one gear closer. Then try again.
