Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The short version: VEX IQ Builder has no server and no accounts of its own. We don't collect, store, sell or share your information. Your builds stay in your browser, or in your own Google Drive if you choose to save them there.

Who we are

VEX IQ Builder ("the app", "we") is a free, open-source web app for designing VEX IQ robots. It is a fan project made by an individual developer and has no connection to VEX Robotics or Innovation First. The source code is public at github.com/adriangonsalves/vexiq-builder.

What stays on your device

The app runs entirely in your web browser. These things are kept only in your browser's own storage on your device, and are never sent to us:

  • The builds you make and save in File → Load…, and their undo history.
  • Your settings, such as view and lighting choices.
  • The copy of the app and part models downloaded for offline use, if you turn that on.
  • If you use Google Drive: a random code that tells your devices apart while editing, the last Drive folder you saved to, and which Drive file your build came from.

You can delete all of this at any time by deleting builds in the app or clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Google Drive (optional)

You can choose to save builds to Google Drive and share them with your team. Only if you choose this:

  • You sign in with Google in a Google pop-up. We never see your password.
  • The app asks only for the drive.file permission. This lets the app open and change only the files it created for you or the files you pick yourself in Google's file picker. It cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • The app reads your Google account's display name so teammates can see who is editing a shared build. While you edit, your display name is written onto that build file in Drive (for example "Locked by Maya") and removed when you choose Done editing. Anyone who can open that file in Drive can see it.
  • The sign-in token Google gives the app is kept in memory only. It lasts about an hour and is gone when you close or reload the page. It is never written to storage or sent anywhere except Google.
  • Your build files travel directly between your browser and Google. They never pass through a server of ours, because there isn't one.

The app does not read your email address, contacts, photos or any other Google data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

VEX IQ Builder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to open, save and lock the build files you work on. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to anyone else, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

To remove the app's access, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose VEX IQ Builder and remove access. Your build files stay in your Drive until you delete them there.

Google's own handling of your account is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies of our own.
  • No accounts, sign-up or email list.
  • No selling or sharing of information with anyone.

Hosting

The app's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service. We don't get access to these. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The app is made for students, including children under 13. Because it collects no personal information, it can be used without signing in. Google Drive sign-in is optional and handled by Google: children under 13 can only sign in with a Google account set up by a parent (through Family Link) or by their school, under Google's own rules. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, at any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting information it doesn't today, the policy will say so before that happens.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/adriangonsalves/vexiq-builder/issues.
