Change how the 3D view looks: flat or deep, one view or four, and how much room it gets.

1. Open the view options. {{Right-click the view cube (1).|Tap **View** [[View]] in the toolbar.}}
   ![The view cube in the corner of the 3D view](shot:looking-around-cube)
2. Choose **Perspective** or **Orthographic** (1). Perspective looks deep. Orthographic looks flat, so lines stay square. {{Press `P` to switch.|}}
   ![The view options menu with Orthographic marked](shot:views-projection)
3. Choose **4-View** (1) to see the front, top and side next to the main view. Choose **Single** to go back.
   ![The view options menu with 4-View marked](shot:views-layout)

## The three small views in 4-View
They are labelled **X · Front**, **Y · Top** and **Z · Side**, and are always flat. They show the same build as the main view, including the part you selected and any Explode. The big view works as usual. The small views only look: {{scroll to zoom and drag to slide. Clicking a part in one selects nothing.|drag to slide. Tapping a part in one selects nothing.}} {{Press `F`|Tap **Focus** [[Focus]]}} to frame all three again.

## Stereo, Cross-eyed, Depth and Full screen
**Stereo** shows two pictures side by side, for your eyes or a phone viewer. You can work in either picture. The view cube is hidden, so open the menu with **View** [[View]] in the toolbar. Stereo is always in perspective.

Stereo adds three more choices. **Cross-eyed** swaps the pictures, for looking cross-eyed. Leave it off for a phone viewer or looking parallel. [[Depth]] slides from 0.25× to 3×. It sets how far apart the two pictures are. **Full screen** fills the screen with the pair. {{Press `Esc` to leave.|A touch screen has no Esc key, so use your browser's own way out of full screen.}}

## The grid
**Grid** [[View → Grid]] shows or hides the floor grid. One square is one pitch, the hole spacing on VEX IQ beams. Choose it again to hide it. The menu stays open so you can change more.

## Wide and Panels
On a computer screen, **Wide** [[Wide]] in the toolbar hides the Library and Outline so the 3D view is bigger. The button then says **Panels**. Select **Panels** [[Panels]] to bring them back. This browser remembers your choice. Phones have no Wide button.
