Your builds save themselves, so there is no Save button. Use **File** to start, switch and back up.

1. {{Click the build's name (1) at the top left, type a new name, then press `Enter`.|The toolbar has no name box here. Choose **File → Load…**, then [[Rename]] on the build.}}
   ![The build's name marked in the toolbar](shot:saving-name)
2. Open **File** [[File]] (1) and choose **New** (2) for an empty build. The old one stays saved.
   ![The File menu with New marked](shot:saving-new)
3. Open **File** (1) and choose **Load…** [[File → Load…]] (2) to see your builds.
   ![The File menu with Load… marked](shot:saving-load)
4. {{Click|Tap}} [[Open]] (1) on the build you want.
   ![The builds window with Open marked](shot:saving-open)
5. Open **File** (1) and choose **Export** (2) to download a copy.
   ![The File menu with Export marked](shot:saving-export)

> **Watch out** Builds live only in this browser, so Export a copy to keep it safe.

## Why to Export
Clearing the browser's data, or using a different browser or computer, loses your builds. Export downloads the build as a file. To bring it back, choose **File → Load…**, then [[Open from computer…]], and pick the file. Then choose [[Open by itself]] to open it as a new build.

## What Load… can do
Each build shows a picture, its name, when it changed and how many parts it has. The one you are in says "Open now".
- [[Open]] switches to that build, or adds it to yours (see below). Double-clicking it works too.
- [[Rename]] (the pencil) lets you type a new name.
- [[Duplicate]] (two squares) makes a copy, named with "copy" at the end.
- [[Delete]] (the bin) asks first, then removes it.
- [[Open from computer…]] opens a build file, or adds it to yours.

You can also rename from the build's name at the top.

## Open by itself, or add to this build
When your build has parts, opening a starter, a saved build or a file asks first.
- [[Open by itself]] opens it as its own build. Yours stays saved.
- [[Add to this build]] puts its parts into your build as one group. The group sits beside your parts and is selected, so you can move it into place.

One undo takes the whole group out again. The build it came from doesn't change.
![The question with Open by itself and Add to this build](shot:saving-open-or-add)

## Build with a teammate
Each of you builds one part of the robot, like the drive base and the claw.
1. Your teammate chooses **File → Export** to save their build as a file.
2. They send you the file, or copy it to your computer.
3. You choose **File → Load…**, then [[Open from computer…]], and pick the file.
4. Choose [[Add to this build]].
5. Drag the new group into place and join it with pins.

The new group is named after the file. Locked parts come in unlocked. If it won't move, a part in it is hidden: use the group's hide button in the Outline. **Ungroup** makes its parts loose again; select some and press `G` for a smaller group.

## Delete this build…
Choose [[File → Delete this build…]] to remove the build you are in. It asks first, and it can't be undone. Then the app opens your most recent other build, or a new empty one.
![The File menu with Delete this build… marked](shot:saving-delete)

## The starters
Choose [[File → Starter]] to start a new build from a ready-made one, or to add one to your build. Your open build stays saved. The list has two parts: **Robots** are whole robots, and **Assemblies** are pieces of a robot.
![The Starter list open in the File menu](shot:saving-starters)

## The assembly starters
**Sample starter** is what a new browser opens with: a beam, a pin, a shaft and a gear.
![Sample starter](shot:saving-starter-sample)

**Ratchet winch** is the winch from Hero Bot Swish: a ratchet and pawl on a swinging arm. In [[Tools → Play mode]], run the motors forward to wind the spool, stop to hold it, and reverse to let it go.

**Simple drivetrain** is the Clawbot's base: chassis, wheels and drive motors, without the brain.
![Simple drivetrain](shot:saving-starter-simple-drivetrain)

**Simple arm** is the Clawbot's tower and arm, with its motor and gears.
![Simple arm](shot:saving-starter-simple-arm)

**Simple claw** is the Clawbot's claw, with its wrist and motor.
![Simple claw](shot:saving-starter-simple-claw)

To build a Clawbot from the pieces, open **Simple drivetrain**. Then add **Simple arm** and **Simple claw** with [[Add to this build]]. Move each group into place, and add a brain.

## When the browser can't save
Some private windows turn off storage. The builds window then says so. **New**, **Starter** and **Export** still work, so Export before you close.
