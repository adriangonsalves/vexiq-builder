## A part turned red
It passes into another part. It goes back to where it was. Move it clear, or use **Tools → Fix assembly**. See [Moving and turning parts](help:moving).

## A part won't join
Select the part first, and bring it near the holes. If the bar says **Manual Join**, nothing is lined up yet. Click or tap it, then pick a hole. See [Joining parts](help:joining).

## Nothing lights up
Lit holes show only for a selected part that is close to a hole it fits. Move it closer. Or use **Mark a hole** on the hole you want. See [Joining parts](help:joining).

## The wheel zooms when I want to step through fits
The wheel steps through fits only while you are picking a hole or have arrows showing. Otherwise it zooms. Start a join first. See [Joining parts](help:joining).

## I can't see a button the guide names
Many buttons show only when they fit what is selected. Select a part, or a group, and look again. A **Show me** chip in the guide points at the button when it is on screen. See [The context bar](help:context-bar).

## My build is gone
Builds live in this browser only. Choose **File → Load…** to see the saved ones. A different browser or a cleared browser has none. Export your builds to keep copies. See [Saving and sharing](help:saving).

## The app is slow
In **Settings**, check that Shadows and Ambient occlusion are off. If the view shows four pictures, right-click the view cube and choose **Single**. Big builds take longer. See [Settings](help:settings).

## Explode leaves some parts in place
The Robot Brain stays still, or the biggest part if there is no brain. See [Explode](help:explode).

## A gear won't turn in Play mode
Open the list at the bottom of the Play panel. It says which parts don't move as built. A gear may be too far from its partner, locked to its shaft, or not on a shaft. See [Play mode](help:play-mode).

## A motor says Stalled
Something it turns is blocked, or two gears ask for different speeds. Free the part, then use the opposite throttle. See [Play mode](help:play-mode).

## The chain sags
The builder adds a link instead of making it too tight. Move a sprocket the way the message says, then build again. See [Chain and track](help:chain-and-track).

## The Chain builder is greyed out
Point at it to read why. You need two or more sprockets, in one plane, with parallel shafts. See [Chain and track](help:chain-and-track).

## A band is refused
The band would hang loose, or the route needs an over or under answer. The message names a size that fits. See [Rubber bands and belts](help:rubber-bands).

## The sheet won't bend
Pin one or two rows flat first, and select the part to curve it onto. If no row fits, the window says why. See [Plastic sheets](help:plastic-sheets).

## It says "Part is locked"
Unlock it in the Outline, then try again. See [The Outline and Properties](help:outline).

## The Audit says a pin is loose
The pin isn't seated in any part. Select its row and press [[Attempt Fix]]. See [Check and fix your build](help:check-and-fix).

## I can't drag a part from the Library on my tablet
That doesn't work by touch yet. Mark a hole, then tap the part in the Library. Close Help first to see the Library. See [Tablets and phones](help:tablets-and-phones).

## Undo does nothing in Play mode
Play mode doesn't change your build, so it has nothing to undo. Press `Esc` to leave, then undo. See [Undo and the timeline](help:undo-history).
