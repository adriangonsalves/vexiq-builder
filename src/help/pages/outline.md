The Outline lists every part in your build. Properties shows the part you picked.

1. {{Find the **Outline** [[Outline]] (1) beside the 3D view.|Close Help, then open the **Outline** [[Outline]] (1).}} Each row is one part.
   ![The Outline with its list of parts marked](shot:outline-list)
2. Type a few letters of a part's name or number in the search box (1). Only matching rows stay.
   ![The Outline with "beam" typed in the search box](shot:outline-search)
3. {{Click|Tap}} a row to pick that part. Its Properties [[Properties]] show.
4. {{Click|Tap}} the eye (1) to hide a part. {{Click|Tap}} the padlock (2) to lock it.
   ![A picked part with the eye and padlock marked](shot:outline-hide-lock)

## Searching the list
The search box looks at a part's label, its catalog name and its number. The title shows how many rows match out of all the parts. {{Press `Esc` or the small cross to clear it.|Tap the small cross to clear it.}} Searching only changes the list. Your picks and your build stay as they are.

## Folders, hide and lock
A group is a folder. Tap its arrow to open or close it. Its number is how many parts it holds. Picking the folder picks the whole group. Picking a row inside picks just that part. The eye and padlock on a folder act on every part inside. A dash means only some parts are hidden or locked. A hidden part stays in your build but is not drawn. A locked part can't be moved.

## Rename a group
Pick the group. In Properties, change the name in the [[Group name]] box, then press `Enter` or tap away. An empty name is refused. `Esc` puts the old name back. The app can't rename a single part yet. Its label stays as it is.

## Locate and the base name
A long list is hard to search by eye. Press [[Locate]] (the small target above the list) and the picked part's row scrolls into view. It opens a closed folder if it has to. Hold [[Locate]] for half a second to keep it following your picks. Hold it again to stop. The picked row also shows its base name: the catalog name and number of the part.

## Checkboxes in Select mode
Choose **Select** [[Select → Select mode]] and turn on Select mode. Every row gets a checkbox. A tap on a row adds or removes it. A folder's checkbox picks the whole group, and a dash means only some of it. {{Hold `Ctrl` (`Cmd` on a Mac) and click to add a row without Select mode. `Shift` + click adds every row between.|On a touch screen, tap **Add to selection** [[Add to selection]] on the context bar to turn Select mode on.}}

## What Properties shows
Its top line names your pick. For one part it gives the label, the catalog name and number, and the group it is in. Below that are the position, the angles, the quarter-turn buttons and the colour squares. A sheet or band also shows its own controls. [[Settings]] has an option for advanced options. Turn it on to add the fastening tools, Mirror and a part readout.

For a group, Properties shows the group name, the centre of the group, nudge buttons, quarter turns, and [[Hide all]] and [[Lock all]]. Several loose parts show the same, with no name box. Typing a centre or tapping a button moves every part together. Clone and the bin act on all of them.

## Outline and Properties on a tablet and phone
Help covers the Outline while it is open, so close Help first. On a tablet the Outline is a tab in the side panel. On a phone it is the **Outline** button along the bottom. It opens over the 3D view. Tap it again to close it, or pin it to keep it open.

Tap a row and Properties take the Outline's place. Tap **Outline** (1) at the top to go back. The list stays where you scrolled it. Clearing your pick also goes back.
