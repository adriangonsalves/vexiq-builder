Here is what each part of the screen is for, on a computer.

- (1) **Toolbar**: undo, selecting, tools, snap, view and file.
- (2) **Build name** and part count: click the name to rename it.
- (3) **Library**: every part you can add.
- (4) **3D view**: where the robot is.
- (5) **Context bar**: buttons for what you can do now.
- (6) **View cube**: click it to look from another side.
- (7) **Outline**: a list of your parts.
- (8) **Properties**: the selected part's position, angles and colour.
- (9) **Messages strip**: what the app just did, and warnings.
- (10) **Shortcuts strip**: the keys that work here.

## On a tablet
The toolbar shows icons only. **Library** and **Outline** are two tabs on the left; Properties takes the Outline's place when you select a part. Help also takes the place of the Library and Outline while it is open, and they come back when you close it. The context bar buttons are bigger.

![The tablet screen with the toolbar, tabs, 3D view, context bar and view cube marked](shot:tour-tablet)

## On a phone
The 3D view fills the screen. The toolbar scrolls sideways. The Library and Outline open as drawers from buttons, and Help fills the whole screen. The context bar sits on the left edge, clear of the view cube. Warnings wait behind a button at the top left.

![The phone screen with the toolbar, 3D view, context bar and view cube marked](shot:tour-phone)

## Buttons at the top of Help
Type in the Search help box to find a page. Press Help home to go back to the list of pages. Press Copy link to copy a web link to the page you are reading, so you can share it.

The Mouse and Touch switch (Wording for: Mouse, Wording for: Touch) changes the steps to fit a mouse or a finger.
