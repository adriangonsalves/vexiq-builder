An AI assistant can build in this app through MCP. These switches show only in the dev app, not on the website.

1. {{Click|Tap}} **Settings** [[Settings]], then turn on **Show advanced options**.
2. Find the **Assistant (MCP)** section. The switch **Let the assistant change the build** is on or off.
3. Look at the top left, next to the build's name. The badge there says whether the assistant is connected.
4. {{Click|Tap}} **History** [[History]]. Marks on the timeline have a color for who made each step.

<!-- more -->

## What the badge says
The badge shows the number of parts. "MCP Live • 5 parts" means an assistant is connected and allowed to change the build. "MCP Off • 5 parts" means the switch is off. Just "5 parts" means no assistant is connected. The badge only shows with advanced options on. Hover over it for its tip.

## What turning the switch off stops
The hint says: "Off: the AI assistant can still look at the build, but can't change it". It can still search parts, read the build and its steps, check it and take pictures. Any change it tries is refused with: "The assistant is turned off in the app's Settings (Assistant). Turn it on there to make changes."

## Colors on the timeline
The bar has a key with four colors: **App** (blue) for your own steps, **MCP** (orange), **Assistant** (purple) and **Smart action** (green). Hover over a mark and the tip names where that step came from. An assistant's step is one step to Undo, like yours.
