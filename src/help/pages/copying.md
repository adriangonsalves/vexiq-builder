Copy a part when you need another one just like it.

1. Pick a part.
2. {{Click [[Duplicate]] [[Duplicate]] (1) on the context bar, or press `D`.|Tap [[Duplicate]] [[Duplicate]] (1) on the context bar.}} A copy appears one step to the side and is picked.
   ![The context bar with Duplicate marked](shot:copying-duplicate)
3. Move the copy where you want it.
4. {{Pick a pin, hold `Ctrl` (`Cmd` on a Mac), point at a hole and double-click.|Pick a pin. Tap **Stamp copies** [[Stamp copies]] (1), then tap a lit hole.}} A copy goes in the hole.
   ![The context bar with Stamp copies marked](shot:copying-stamp)

<!-- more -->

## Stamp copies and its fits
Stamp copies shows only for a part with pins, pegs or shafts. {{Holding `Ctrl` lights the button for you. Rings show every hole the copy would go in. A grey ring means the hole is taken.|The button stays on, so you can stamp again and again. Tap it again, or change your pick, to stop.}} A part can fit a hole more than one way. {{Scroll the mouse wheel to try the other fits.|The wheel bar shows how many fits there are, such as "2 / 6". Step through them, then tap the tick.}}

Your first part stays where it is and stays picked. Each copy keeps its colour and gets the next free number in its name. One Undo removes one copy.

If the hole is taken, the app says "That hole already has a part in it". If something blocks the way, it says "The way in is blocked". Neither adds a copy.

## Copying a group
Pick a whole group, or several parts, and use [[Duplicate]]. They are copied together, one step to the side, with their places kept. A copied group is named after the old one with "copy" on the end. A band copied with its parts stays on the copies. One Undo removes the lot.

## Mirror makes the other side
A copy is the same way round. For a left side and a right side, use Mirror instead. See [Advanced tools](help:advanced-tools).
