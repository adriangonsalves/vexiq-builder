The app fits itself to your screen. Everything a mouse does is on the screen by touch too.

1. Open the Library or the Outline. On a tablet, tap its tab, **Library** (1) or **Outline** (2). On a phone, tap its button at the bottom.
   ![The tablet screen with the Library and Outline tabs marked](shot:tablet-layout)
2. Turn the view with one finger. Zoom and slide it with two fingers.
3. Tap a part to select it.
4. Use the context bar for what keys and right-click do on a computer.

> **Tip** Press and hold a context bar button to see its name.

<!-- more -->

## The three layouts
A computer (wider than about 1180 pixels) has the Library and Outline beside the 3D view. A tablet has one side panel with Library and Outline tabs. A phone has the 3D view across the whole screen. Turning the device switches layouts and keeps your build.

## The icon toolbar
On a tablet and phone the toolbar shows icons only. Press and hold one to see its name. On a phone it scrolls sideways. Select, Tools, Snap and File open their menus from their icons. **View** opens the view options, since there may be no right mouse button.

## Tabs, drawers and pins
On a tablet, tap a part in the Outline and its Properties show in place of the Outline. Tap **← Outline** to go back. On a phone, the Library and Outline open as drawers. A pin keeps a drawer open, with the 3D view shrinking above it. An unpinned drawer closes when you tap the view. Pins are remembered in this browser. Help takes the place of the Library and Outline on a tablet and fills the phone screen, so close Help to see the Library.

## What the context bar does for you
It stands in for held keys, the mouse wheel, right-click, double-click and hovering. Tap a hole to light it before a part goes in. See [The context bar](help:context-bar).

## What can't be done by touch yet
- Dragging a part from the Library into the view. Instead, mark a hole, then tap the part in the Library. See [Finding and adding parts](help:library).
- Choosing the working half in Stereo.
- Pinching or sliding the small views in 4-View.

## Messages on a phone
Warnings and errors wait behind a button at the top left of the 3D view. Tap it to read them, and tap it again to hide them. The Wide view isn't offered on a phone.
