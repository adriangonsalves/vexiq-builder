## Looking around
- {{`P`|Choose **View**}}: switch between Perspective and Orthographic.
- {{`1` `2` `3`|Tap a face of the view cube}}: look from the front, top or side.
- {{`Shift` + `1` `2` `3`|Double-tap a face of the view cube}}: look from the opposite side.
- {{`F`|Tap **Focus**}}: frame the selected part, or the whole build.
- {{Drag empty space|Drag with one finger}}: turn the view.
- {{Right-drag|Drag with two fingers}}: slide the view.
- {{Mouse wheel|Pinch with two fingers}}: zoom.
- {{Right-click the view cube|Tap **View**}}: open the view options.

## Selecting
- {{Click a part|Tap a part}}: select it. A grouped part selects its whole group.
- {{`Shift` + click|Tap **Add to selection**, then tap parts}}: add a part or take it out.
- {{`Shift` + `Alt` + click|Tap **One part of a group**, then tap}}: pick one part inside a group.
- {{`Shift` + wheel|Tap **Grow selection**}}: grow or shrink the selection by joints.
- {{`B`|Choose **Select → Box select**}}: box select on or off.
- {{`C`|Choose **Select → Select Connected**}}: select what is joined to the part.
- {{`Ctrl` + `A` or `Cmd` + `A`|Choose **Select → Select All**}}: select every part.
- {{`G`|Choose **Select → Group**}}: group the selected parts.
- {{`Shift` + `G`|Choose **Select → Ungroup**}}: split a group.
- {{`Esc`|Choose **Select → Select None**}}: select nothing.

## Moving and turning
- {{`W`|Tap **Move**}}: slide the part along its arrows.
- {{`E`|Tap **Rotate**}}: turn the part with its rings.
- {{Right-click the part|Tap **Move** or **Rotate**}}: switch between Move and Rotate.
- {{Hold `Alt`|Tap **World arrows**}}: arrows follow the room, not the part.
- {{Hold `Alt` while dragging|Tap **Free move**}}: the part stops jumping onto holes.
- {{Wheel on a selected ring|Use the thumb wheel on the bar}}: turn one step.

## Joining
- {{Double-click the part|Tap **Auto Join** or **Manual Join**}}: join the part.
- {{`Alt` + click a hole|Tap **Mark a hole**, then tap the hole}}: pick the hole the next part goes into.
- {{`R` and `Shift` + `R`, or the wheel|Tap [[Next]] and [[Previous]]}}: step through the fits.
- {{Click a lit hole|Tap the hole, then the tick}}: put the part in the fit shown.
- {{`Ctrl` + double-click a hole|Tap **Stamp copies**}}: insert a copy there. `Cmd` works on a Mac.
- {{`Enter`|Tap the tick}}: keep a shown bend.
- {{`Esc`|Tap [[Cancel]]}}: stop picking holes or hide the arrows.

## Copying, deleting and undoing
- {{`D`|Tap [[Duplicate]]}}: copy the selected part.
- {{`Del` or `Backspace`|Tap [[Delete]]}}: remove it. Undo brings it back.
- {{`Ctrl` + `Z` or `Cmd` + `Z`|Tap **Undo**}}: undo.
- {{`Shift` + `Ctrl` + `Z`, or `Ctrl` + `Y`|Tap [[Redo]]}}: redo. `Cmd` + `Y` does nothing.

## Tools and Help
- {{`X`|Choose **Tools → Explode**}}: open or close Explode.
- {{`Esc` in Play mode|Tap the cross on the Play panel}}: leave Play mode.
- {{`Enter` in the Band builder|Tap [[Done]]}}: finish the band.
- {{`Backspace` in the Band builder|Tap **Back**}}: take off the last spot.
- {{`?`|Tap **Help**}}: open or close Help.

## When keys don't work
Letter keys do nothing while you type in a box or while Settings is open. In Play mode only `Esc`, `P` and `1` `2` `3` work.

Press `Esc` again for the next job. It leaves full screen first, then Play mode, then hides arrows, then closes Explode. After that it turns off a bar tool, and last it deselects.
