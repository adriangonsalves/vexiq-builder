The context bar is a column of buttons by the 3D view. It shows what you can do right now.

1. Look for the column of buttons at the edge of the 3D view [[Context bar]] (1).
   ![The context bar beside the 3D view with a part selected](shot:context-bar-where)
2. Select a part. More buttons appear, such as [[Duplicate]] and [[Delete]].
3. {{Rest the pointer on a button|Press and hold a button}} to see its name and what it does. {{|Lifting your finger does not press it.}}
   ![A tip open above the Duplicate button](shot:context-bar-tip)
4. A greyed button can't be used now. Its tip says why.

> **Tip** On a tablet or phone the bar does what keys, right-click and double-click do on a computer.

<!-- more -->

## Where the bar is in each layout
On a computer and a tablet it runs down the right edge, under the view cube. On a phone it sits on the left edge, under the warning button, clear of the cube. Buttons are bigger on touch screens. A short view uses more columns instead of hiding buttons.

## Buttons that are always there
- **Move** [[Move]]: slide the part along its arrows. {{`W`|}}
- **Rotate** [[Rotate]]: turn it with rings. {{`E` or right-click the part|}}
- **Add to selection** [[Add to selection]]: tap parts to add or remove them. {{`Shift` + click|}}
- **Mark a hole** [[Mark a hole]]: pick the hole the next part goes in. {{`Alt` + click|}}

## Buttons for selected parts
- **Auto Join** and **Manual Join** [[Join]]: join the part. {{Double-click the part|}}
- **Grow selection** [[Grow selection]]: add the parts joined to this one. {{`Shift` + wheel|}}
- [[Duplicate]] [[Duplicate]]: copy it. {{`D`|}}
- [[Delete]] [[Delete]]: remove it. Undo brings it back. {{`Del`|}}
- **One part of a group** [[One part of a group]]: pick a single part inside a group. {{`Shift` + `Alt` + click|}}

## Buttons for one part
- **Stamp copies** [[Stamp copies]]: put a copy in each hole you pick. {{`Ctrl`/`Cmd` + double-click|}}
- **World arrows** [[World arrows]]: arrows follow the room. {{Hold `Alt`|}}
- **Free move** [[Free move]]: stop parts jumping onto holes. {{Hold `Alt` while dragging|}}
- **Turn step** [[Turn step]]: how far each turn goes (Rotate only).

## Buttons that stay on and buttons for one use
Move, Rotate, Add to selection, World arrows, Free move and Stamp copies stay on until you tap them again or press {{`Esc`|the cross}}. **Mark a hole** and **One part of a group** work for one tap, then switch off. {{Holding a key lights its button while you hold it.|}}

## The wheel bar
While the wheel steps through choices instead of zooming, the buttons give way to a thumb wheel. [[Wheel]] turns it, [[Next]] and [[Previous]] step one, and [[Pins only]] limits Grow selection. [[Cancel]] stops with nothing changed. {{`R` is Next, `Shift` + `R` is Previous, `Esc` is Cancel.|Drag the wheel up for next.}}

## The tick buttons
The tick ends a step. It reads **Put it in**, **Put a copy in**, **Keep**, [[Done]] or **Add this spot**, depending on the job. It is greyed with "Tap a hole first" until a hole is lit.
