Blueprint draws your robot from the top, the side and the front, like a plan.

1. Choose **Tools → Blueprint** [[Tools → Blueprint]] (1). The drawing opens in a window.
   ![The Tools menu with Blueprint marked](shot:blueprint-menu)
2. Select [[Open Full SVG]] (1) to see it big in a new browser tab.
   ![The Blueprint window with Open Full SVG marked](shot:blueprint-dialog)
3. Select **Close** (1) when you are done.
   ![The Blueprint window with Close marked](shot:blueprint-close)

<!-- more -->

## What the three views show
The drawing has a top view, a side view and a front view of the whole build. Beside them it lists the robot's size and the result of a quick check. Blueprint is greyed out until the build has a part.

## Printing it
Blueprint has no print button. Open the drawing with [[Open Full SVG]], then print that browser tab. {{Press `Ctrl` + `P` (`Cmd` + `P` on a Mac), or use the browser's Print menu.|Use your browser's share or print menu.}}
