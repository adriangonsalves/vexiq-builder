Save the app and every part on this device, then build with no internet.

1. {{Click|Tap}} **Settings** [[Settings]] (1).
   ![The toolbar with Settings marked](shot:settings-gear)
2. Turn on **Use offline** (1).
   ![The Offline section with the Use offline switch marked](shot:offline-switch)
3. Check the size, then {{click|tap}} **Download** (1).
   ![The size check with Download marked](shot:offline-size)
4. Wait for the bar to fill. [[Cancel]] (1) stops it.
   ![The download bar with Cancel marked](shot:offline-progress)
5. When it says **Available offline** (1), you are done.
   ![The Offline section saying Available offline](shot:offline-done)

> **Watch out** Builds stay in this browser, so [Export a copy](help:saving#why-to-export) too.

## If a download stops
[[Cancel]] stops the download. The section then says "Download stopped: Download cancelled." Press **Resume** to carry on from the files already saved.

A download can also stop on its own. The section tells you why, for example "No internet connection. Connect and try again." or "There isn’t enough space on this device." Fix that, then press **Resume**.

Before the download starts, the section shows its size, about 100 MB, and the free space. [[Cancel]] there saves nothing.

## Install on Chrome or Edge
When the download ends, the section shows how to install. You can also press **Install the app** at the bottom.

1. Press **Install app**.
2. Answer the browser's question.

The app then opens in its own window, from your apps, with no internet. If your browser can't install it, the section says so. The site still works offline in that browser.

The installed app may ask "Use VEX IQ without internet?" if you haven't downloaded yet. Press **Download**, or **Not now**. After two Not nows it stops asking.

## Install on an iPad or iPhone
The Home Screen app keeps its own copy, apart from Safari. So add it to the Home Screen first, then download from there. When you turn on Use offline in Safari, it shows these steps:
1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar. In other browsers it is in the browser's menu.
2. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open VEX IQ from the Home Screen.
4. Tap **Download** when the app offers to work without internet.

**Download in Safari anyway** skips this and downloads in Safari. [[Cancel]] goes back.

Builds made in Safari don't appear in the Home Screen app. In Safari choose [[File → Export]]. In the app choose **File → Load… → Open from computer…**.

## Install with Safari on a Mac
1. In Safari's menu bar, choose **File → Add to Dock…**
2. Open VEX IQ from the Dock or Launchpad.
3. Choose **Download** when the app offers to work without internet.

## New versions
After a download, the app checks for a newer version when it opens and when you come back online. A bar says "A new version of VEX IQ Builder is out. Download it to keep using it offline." The section says "Update needed."

Press **Download update**, or **Later**. Until you update, the app keeps working offline with the old version. When the update is done, the app reloads into it. Your build is saved first.

## Turn it off
Turn **Use offline** off. The app asks "Remove the offline copy? The app will need the internet again. Your builds stay." Your builds stay saved in this browser.

## Seeing and keeping what you have
**Available offline** also shows next to the build's name. Select it to open the Offline section.

Your builds live only in this browser on this device. Use [[File → Export]] as your backup, as [Saving and sharing](help:saving#why-to-export) explains. Some browsers clear saved data if the app isn't used for a while. The section warns you when this one may. Installing the app makes it less likely.
