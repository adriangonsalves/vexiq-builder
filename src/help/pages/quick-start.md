Join two beams with a pin. It takes about five minutes.

1. Open **File** [[File]] (1) and choose **New** (2) for an empty build.
   ![The File menu open with New marked](shot:qs-new)
2. {{Type "2x8 beam" in the Library search box (1), then click **+ Add** (2).|Close Help to see the Library. Type "2x8 beam" in its search box (1), then tap **+ Add** (2).}}
   ![The Library with 2x8 beam typed in the search box and + Add marked](shot:qs-beam)
3. {{Drag with the left button to turn the view. Drag with the right button to slide it. Scroll to zoom.|Drag with one finger to turn the view. Drag with two fingers to slide it. Pinch to zoom.}}
   ![One beam in the 3D view](shot:qs-look)
4. Add a "0x2 Connector Pin" the same way. It appears next to the beam.
   ![A pin next to the beam](shot:qs-pin)
5. {{Click|Tap}} **Auto Join** [[Auto Join]] (1) on the context bar. The pin slides into the beam.
   ![The context bar with Auto Join marked](shot:qs-autojoin)
6. Add a second "2x8 beam". It appears in the air, apart from the first.
   ![A second beam floating above the first](shot:qs-beam2)
7. {{Click **Manual Join** [[Manual Join]] (1), then click the end of the pin.|Tap **Manual Join** (1), tap the end of the pin, then tap the tick.}} The beam moves onto the pin.
   ![The context bar with Manual Join marked, and the second beam in the air](shot:qs-manual)
8. {{Click|Tap}} **Undo** [[Undo]] (1) to go back a step, and [[Redo]] (2) to bring it back. {{On a keyboard, `Ctrl` + `Z` also undoes.|}}
   ![The Undo and Redo buttons marked](shot:qs-undo)
