Settings changes how the 3D view looks and turns on extra tools.

1. {{Click|Tap}} **Settings** [[Settings]] (1), the gear at the right end of the toolbar.
   ![The toolbar with Settings marked](shot:settings-gear)
2. {{Click|Tap}} a switch under **Display** (1), such as [[Shadows]]. It changes at once.
   ![The Settings window with Shadows marked](shot:settings-display)
3. Close the window with the **X** (1). {{You can also press `Esc` or click outside it.|You can also tap outside it.}}
   ![The top of the Settings window with the X marked](shot:settings-close)

> **Tip** Closing the window never undoes a change.

## What the Display switches do
- [[Shadows]] adds soft shadows from the main light. It starts off in a new build.
- [[Ambient occlusion]] darkens creases and inside corners. It uses more graphics power, so turn it off if the app is slow. It starts off in a new build.
- [[Environment reflections]] adds studio lighting and shine on metal shafts and surfaces. It starts on in a new build.
- [[Reflection brightness]] sets how strong the shine is, from 0% to 200%. It starts at 75% in a new build. It is greyed out while Environment reflections is off.

Each build keeps its own Display choices.

## What Show advanced options adds
It starts off. To turn it on, open Settings and use **Show advanced options** under **Advanced options**. Two things appear.

- In Properties, when one part is selected: **Smart Assembly & Fastening** and a part information section. The first holds **Auto-Fasten Joint**, **Snap & Mate to Port**, the gear train builder and Mirror. The second shows the part's number, size and fit. See [Advanced tools](help:advanced-tools).
- In Settings, a choice under **History**: **Linear** or **Keep branches**. See [History branches](help:history-branches). With the switch off, Settings only says that undo and redo work as usual.

Turn the switch off to hide the tools again. Choose **Linear** first if you want branches off, because **Keep branches** stays on when the switch goes off.

## What this browser remembers
The app saves these on this device, in this browser only:
- Your builds, with each build's Display choices.
- The advanced options switch and the History choice.
- Whether you turned on Use offline.
- Whether the panels are hidden, which drawers are pinned on a tablet or phone, and some Outline choices.
- Library list or grid, and your Recent parts.
- Whether the history timeline shows.
- Help's Mouse or Touch choice, and that you have seen the welcome card.

Another browser or device starts fresh. Clearing the browser's data erases all of it, so [Export a copy](help:saving#why-to-export) of builds you care about. To use the app with no internet, see [Use it without internet](help:offline).
