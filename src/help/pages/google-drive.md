Keep one build on Google Drive so your whole team can work on it. One person edits at a time.

1. Open **File** [[File]] (1) and choose **Save to Google Drive…** [[File → Save to Google Drive…]] (2).
   ![The File menu with Save to Google Drive… marked](shot:drive-save-menu)
2. Choose **Choose a folder…** (1) and pick your team's folder. Or choose **My Drive** (2).
   ![The question with Choose a folder… and My Drive marked](shot:drive-save-where)
3. After you change something, select **Save to Drive** on the [[Drive button]] (1){{, or press `Ctrl`+`S`|}}.
   ![The Drive button reading Save to Drive](shot:drive-save-button)
4. When you stop, select **Done editing** (1). Now a teammate can edit.
   ![The Drive button reading Done editing](shot:drive-done-editing)
5. Your teammate chooses **File → Load…**, then [[Open from Google Drive…]] (1).
   ![The builds window with Open from Google Drive… marked](shot:drive-open)

> **Tip** Share the file in Google Drive itself: keep it in a folder you share with your team.

## Share the file with your team
The app doesn't share files. Google Drive does that.
1. In Google Drive, make a folder for your team.
2. Share the folder with your teammates as **Editor**.
3. Move the build file into the folder.

Then save builds straight into it: **Choose a folder…** lists folders shared with you. Next time, **Save in** and the folder's name is the first choice. A file in **My Drive** can be moved into the folder in Google Drive later.

Each teammate opens it once with [[Open from Google Drive…]]. They choose **Shared with me**, open the folder, and pick the file. A teammate shared as **Viewer** can only look.

## When a teammate is editing
The build opens read-only. The [[Drive button]] (1) says who is editing.
You can look around, use **Explode** (2) and **Play** (3), but you can't change it.
![The toolbar with Read-only · Locked by Maya, Explode and Play marked](shot:drive-read-only)

When they save, your view updates by itself. It waits until you leave Play mode and close Explode. When they choose **Done editing**, a message asks to open in edit mode. Choose **Edit build** to start editing.

## Reconnect to Drive
After you reload the page, a Drive build waits for you. The banner has **Reconnect to Drive** (1) and **Work on this device only** (2).
![The banner with Reconnect to Drive and Work on this device only](shot:drive-paused)

**Reconnect to Drive** signs you in again and picks up where you were. Your changes are still there. If the Wi-Fi drops while you edit, keep going. Your changes stay on this device, and the Drive button says **Reconnect to Drive**.

## Work on this device only
Choose [[Work on this device only]] when Google Drive can't be reached. Your build and all your changes stay, and you can edit again. The build is no longer on Drive, and the file on Drive doesn't change. To share it again, choose **Save to Google Drive…**. That makes a new file.

## If a teammate saved first
Say your tablet slept while a teammate edited and saved. The app won't save over their work. It asks what to do:
- **Save as a copy on Drive** saves your version as a copy, next to theirs.
- **Load latest from Drive** opens their version. Yours is kept in **File → Load…** with "(my changes)" after its name.
- **Cancel** changes nothing.

No choice loses your changes.

## Leaving a build you changed
Opening another build asks first when your changes aren't on Drive yet. Choose **Save and leave**, **Leave without saving**, or **Cancel**. **Leave without saving** keeps your changes on this device.

## What you need
A Google account, like a Gmail account. A school account works if your school allows the app. Google Drive needs the internet, but the rest of the app works without it. Shared drives aren't supported yet, so use a shared folder.
