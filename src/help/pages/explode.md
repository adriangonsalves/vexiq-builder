Explode spreads your robot apart, so you can see every part and how it goes together.

1. Choose **Tools → Explode** [[Tools → Explode]] (1). {{Or press `X`.|}} A ring fills while the app works out how to take it apart.
   ![The Tools menu with Explode marked](shot:explode-menu)
2. The parts slide apart on their own. Drag the slider (1) to go back and forth.
   ![The Explode panel with its slider marked](shot:explode-panel)
3. To put every part back, select the close button (1). {{Or press `X` or `Esc`.|}}
   ![The Explode panel with its close button marked](shot:explode-close)

<!-- more -->

## What moves as one piece
Each piece slides out the way it really comes off, along a pin or shaft. The Robot Brain stays still, or the biggest part if there is no brain. Parts held on a piece go with it. A pin or standoff stays in the part it sits deepest in, and a tire stays on its hub. A chain or track moves as one. Groups move as one in a planned explode. There is nothing to choose: Explode takes the build apart every way.

## Big builds spread out
A build of more than 300 parts spreads out instead. So does one where the app can't move at least half the parts along their pins. Each group moves away from the middle as one piece first. Then single parts move straight out, the further the further out they start. Nothing moves down. The ring may take a few seconds.

## Nothing is changed or saved
Exploding never moves a part for real. Closing the panel puts every part back, and History gets no new step. While it is open you can select and hide parts and look around, but you can't move or turn parts.
