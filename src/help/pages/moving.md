Select a part, then drag its arrows to slide it or its rings to turn it.

1. Select a part. Its arrows appear, with **Move** [[Move]] (1) lit on the context bar.
   ![A beam selected with its three arrows and Move marked on the context bar](shot:moving-arrows)
2. Drag an arrow. The part slides one step at a time.
3. Choose the step size with **Snap** [[Snap]] (1): 1P, 0.5P (2), 0.25P or Free. A pitch (P) is the gap between two holes, 12.7 mm.
   ![The Snap menu open with Snap and 0.5P marked](shot:moving-snap)
4. {{Click **Rotate** [[Rotate]] (1) or press `E`.|Tap **Rotate** [[Rotate]] (1).}} Rings appear. Drag a ring to turn the part.
   ![A beam with its rings and Rotate marked on the context bar](shot:moving-rings)
5. {{Click **Move** or press `W`|Tap **Move**}} to get the arrows back.

> **Tip** {{Right-click the part to switch between Move and Rotate.|Move and Rotate are one tap apart on the context bar.}}

## Arrows and the flat squares
Each arrow slides the part along one line. The small flat squares between the arrows slide it across a flat plane. {{Drag with the left mouse button.|Drag with one finger.}}

## What a pitch is
A pitch is the space between two holes, 12.7 mm or half an inch. **Snap** sets the step: 1P, 0.5P, 0.25P or Free. With Free the part follows your pointer exactly.

## When a part touches another
A part stops when it touches another part, and its holes pull onto nearby holes. A part you drag or turn so it would overlap another goes back where it was, and a message says so. {{Hold `Alt` to turn the pulling off.|Tap **Free move** [[Free move]] to turn the pulling off.}}

## Turning with rings
A free part has three rings: red, green and blue. A part held by one pin gets one orange ring that swings it round the pin. Click a ring to pick it, then turn the wheel to turn in steps. Click the angle label to type an angle. **Turn step** [[Turn step]] sets the step: 15°, 45°, 90° or Free.

## World arrows
The arrows follow the part. Tap **World arrows** [[World arrows]] to make them follow the room instead. {{Holding `Alt` does the same.|}}

## Typing a position, nudging and colour
Properties shows the position in millimetres and the angles. Type a number, or use the -X, +X, -Y, +Y, -Z, +Z buttons to nudge by one step. The Rotate X +90°, Rotate Y +90° and Rotate Z +90° buttons turn a part a quarter turn about that axis. The colour squares change its colour.

## When a part overlaps, and changing colour
An overlapping part turns grey and see-through, and a note appears at the top of the 3D view. Revert Pose puts the part back where it last fitted. For a part you just added, the same button says Cancel Placement and removes it.

Next to the colour squares, Custom color (Custom Color in Properties) opens a picker for any colour you like.
