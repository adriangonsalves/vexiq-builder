Build a driven wheel in the order the parts really go on. An axle is the same thing as a shaft.

1. Push a motor shaft through the plate. Its stop ring goes on the motor side.
2. Put the Smart Motor on four pins around the shaft hole. Its drive socket lines up with the shaft.
3. On the other side, slide on a 0.25x Pitch Spacer until it touches the plate.
4. Slide the wheel along the shaft until it touches the spacer.
5. Push a Rubber Shaft Collar on last, all the way in, until it touches the wheel.
6. Join each part as shown on [Joining parts](help:joining).
   ![The Speedbot with its motors, shafts, spacers, wheels and collars](shot:wheels-speedbot)

> **Watch out** Never fit a motor shaft with the stop ring at the wheel end.

## Plain shafts, motor shafts and the stop ring
A motor shaft, like the 2x Pitch Motor Shaft, has a stop ring near one end. The ring can't pass through a hole. The short end past the ring goes into the motor's socket. The long end comes out the other side of the plate and carries the wheel or gear.

Put the shaft through the plate before the motor goes on. Then the ring sits between the plate and the motor, and the shaft can't fall out. If the shaft goes in with the ring the wrong way round, step through the other fits, as shown in [Stepping through fits](help:joining#stepping-through-fits). A plain shaft has no ring.

## A free axle
A motor shaft with no motor is a free axle. Put its stop ring against one face of the beam or plate it goes through. The long end carries the wheel.

## Spacers and pushing a wheel to the plate
A 0.25x Pitch Spacer goes on the shaft between the plate and the wheel, so the wheel clears the plate. Slide it on and push it to the plate. Then slide the wheel on until it touches the spacer, or the plate if there is no spacer.

## Tires on hubs
A tire goes on a hub, such as the Tire (200 mm Travel) on the Small Wheel Hub (44 mm). The two overlap in the 3D view on purpose. The check does not count that as a problem.

A hub faces its open dish outward, away from the robot.

## Omni wheels and the lock beam
<!-- answers: omni-wheel -->
On the omni wheel, the collar sits in the wheel's centre recess. The recess faces outward, away from the robot. The pin holes are on the inner face.

A 2x2 Center Offset Round Lock Beam goes into the recess from the outside. Its pins push inward into the wheel.

## Rubber collars go on last
Add each Rubber Shaft Collar after the wheel, gear or spacer. Push it all the way in until it touches them. Then nothing slides along the shaft.

## Mounting a Smart Motor on four pins
Lay the motor flat against the plate. Put four 1x1 Connector Pins through the plate around the shaft hole. Line up the motor's drive socket with the shaft.

## The brains and the battery
<!-- answers: brain -->
The Library has the VEX IQ Robot Brain Gen 2 as one piece. It also has the VEX IQ Robot Brain Gen 1 and the VEX IQ Robot Battery.

The Gen 1 battery is its own part. It sits in the bay under the brain. To slide it in, select it and use **Auto Join**: see [Joining parts](help:joining).

For a build that doesn't need them apart, use the VEX IQ Robot Brain Gen 1 with Battery.
