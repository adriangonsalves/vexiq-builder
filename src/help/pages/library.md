Find a part in the Library and add it to your build.

1. {{Type a name or part number in the Library search box (1).|Close Help to see the Library. Type a name or part number in its search box (1).}}
   ![The Library with 2x8 beam typed in the search box](shot:library-search)
2. Pick a category (1) to see only those parts.
   ![The Library with a category button marked](shot:library-category)
3. {{Click|Tap}} a part (1) to add it. It appears near the middle of the 3D view.
   ![The Library with one part card marked](shot:library-add)

> **Tip** The number beside each category is how many parts it holds.

## Categories and Recent
The first button, **Recent**, holds the parts you added lately. The ones you use most stay on top, up to 20. **All** shows every part. The other buttons are the kinds of part, such as Beams & Plates. Each shows its count.

On a tablet or phone the category buttons fold into one button that shows the chosen category. Tap it to pick another.

Recent is kept in this browser and is the same for every build.

## List and Grid
The two small buttons at the top of the Library switch the look. List shows one part per row, with its name, part number and size. Grid shows only pictures, three to a row, each with its longest side.

![The Library in Grid view with the Grid button marked](shot:library-grid)

The Library remembers your choice in this browser.

## Seeing a part's name, size and picture
{{Rest the pointer on a part.|This works with a mouse only.}} After a moment, a small label shows its name, longest side and part number. After about a second, a big picture appears above it.

## Dragging a part into the view
<!-- answers: placing -->
{{Press on a part and drag it into the 3D view. A label with its name follows the pointer.|A part can't be dragged from the Library by touch yet. Mark a hole instead, as shown in "Adding by touch".}}

Where the pointer enters the view, the part is added and follows the pointer. Near a hole, it sits in the first way it fits. Scroll the wheel, or press `R` or `Shift` + `R`, to step through the other ways. Click to put it in.

Click away from any hole to put it down where it is. Press `Esc` to take it away.

## Adding onto a marked hole
Mark a hole first. Then click a part in the Library. It appears already in the hole, in its best fit. The part stays there while you choose.

Pins, shafts and wheels go into the hole. Beams and plates lie on it, with one of their corner holes over it. Step through the other fits, then click in the view to keep it. If nothing fits, nothing is added and a message says why.

## Adding by touch
To put a part into a hole by touch, mark the hole first.

1. Tap **Mark a hole** [[Mark a hole]] on the context bar.
2. Tap the hole or the end of a pin.
3. Close Help, then open the Library. On a tablet, use its tab. On a phone, use the Library button at the bottom.
4. Tap the part.
5. Tap the tick [[Tick]] to keep it, or the cross to cancel.

With a mouse, hold `Alt` and click a hole instead.
