Turn, slide and zoom the 3D view to see your build from any side.

1. {{Drag with the left button to turn the view. Drag with the right button to slide it. Scroll to zoom.|Drag with one finger to turn the view. Drag with two fingers to slide it. Pinch to zoom.}}
   ![A beam in the 3D view, seen from an angle](shot:looking-around-view)
2. {{Click|Tap}} **Focus** [[Focus]] (1) to fit the selected part, or everything, in view. {{Or press `F`.|}}
   ![The toolbar with Focus marked](shot:looking-around-focus)
3. {{Click|Tap}} a face of the view cube (1) to look from that side.
   ![The view cube in the corner of the 3D view](shot:looking-around-cube)

## Using the view cube
The view cube sits in the top right corner of the 3D view and turns as you turn the view. Four faces are named Front, Back, Top and Bottom. The other two say +Z and −Z.

{{Click|Tap}} a face to look straight at it. {{Click|Tap}} an edge to look between two faces. {{Click|Tap}} a corner for a three-quarter view. {{Double-click|Double-tap}} any of them to look from the opposite side.

Drag on the cube to turn the view, as you do on empty space. The cube is hidden in Stereo.

## Front, top and side with the number keys
Press `1` to look from the front, `2` from the top and `3` from the side. Hold `Shift` with the number to look from the opposite side. The view turns smoothly and keeps what you were looking at.

On a touch screen, use the view cube instead.

## Where the view cube's menu is
The menu holds Perspective or Orthographic, Single, 4-View or Stereo, and the Grid and Axes switches.

{{Right-click the view cube to open it. In Stereo, the cube is hidden, so use **View** [[View]] in the toolbar instead.|Tap **View** [[View]] in the toolbar. It is always there on tablets and phones.}}

## More about Focus
Focus frames the selected part. With nothing selected, it frames the whole build. In 4-View, it also frames the three small views again.
