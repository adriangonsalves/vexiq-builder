## Parts and sizes
- **Pitch**: the space between two holes, 12.7 mm or half an inch. See [Moving and turning parts](help:moving).
- **Beam** and **plate**: flat parts with rows of holes. See [Pins, standoffs and connectors](help:pins-and-connectors).
- **Pin**: pushes through holes to hold parts together. See [Pins, standoffs and connectors](help:pins-and-connectors).
- **Standoff**: a short post that holds two parts apart. See [Pins, standoffs and connectors](help:pins-and-connectors).
- **Connector**: a corner part with pegs. See [Pins, standoffs and connectors](help:pins-and-connectors).
- **Shaft**: a rod that carries wheels and gears. An **axle** is the same thing. See [Shafts, wheels and motors](help:shafts-and-wheels).
- **Bore**: the hole in the middle of a gear or wheel that a shaft goes through. See [Gears](help:gears).
- **Collar**: a rubber ring that stops a part sliding along a shaft. See [Shafts, wheels and motors](help:shafts-and-wheels).
- **Spacer**: a thin ring that keeps a wheel off a plate. See [Shafts, wheels and motors](help:shafts-and-wheels).
- **Hub**: the part a tire goes on. See [Shafts, wheels and motors](help:shafts-and-wheels).

## Turning things
- **Gear** and **mesh**: gears mesh when their teeth fit. See [Gears](help:gears).
- **Sprocket**: a toothed wheel for chain and track. See [Chain and track](help:chain-and-track).
- **Tooth gap**: the space between two teeth, where a band can hook on. See [Rubber bands and belts](help:rubber-bands).
- **Pulley**: a wheel with a groove for a band. See [Rubber bands and belts](help:rubber-bands).
- **Stretch**: how much longer a band is than when it hangs free. See [Rubber bands and belts](help:rubber-bands).
- [[Throttle]]: the slider that sets a motor's speed. See [Play mode](help:play-mode).
- **Stalled**: a motor that has stopped because something blocks it. See [Play mode](help:play-mode).

## Putting things together
- **Mate**: to join a part to holes it lines up with. See [Joining parts](help:joining).
- **Fit**: one way a part can go in. See [Joining parts](help:joining).
- **Lit hole**: a hole with an orange ring that a part lines up with. See [Joining parts](help:joining).
- **Overlap**: two parts pass into each other. See [Check and fix your build](help:check-and-fix).
- **Tight fit**: parts that touch with almost no gap, which is fine. See [Check and fix your build](help:check-and-fix).
- **Group**: parts that move as one. See [Selecting and groups](help:selecting).
- **Starter**: a ready-made build to begin from. See [Saving and sharing](help:saving).

## The screen
- **Context bar**: the buttons beside the 3D view for the selected part. See [The context bar](help:context-bar).
- **View cube**: the little cube that turns the view. See [Looking around](help:looking-around).
- **Outline**: the list of parts in your build. See [The Outline and Properties](help:outline).
- **Properties**: the panel about the selected part. See [The Outline and Properties](help:outline).
- **Timeline**: the bar that replays your steps. See [Undo and the timeline](help:undo-history).
- **Audit**: the check that lists problems. See [Check and fix your build](help:check-and-fix).
