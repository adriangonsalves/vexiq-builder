## Purpose

Says what the Help guide must contain: which pages exist and what each one covers, how they are written so a middle schooler can follow them, how their pictures are made, and the checks that keep them complete and true as the app changes.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The guide's pages
The guide SHALL have the pages below, in these groups and this order. Each page SHALL cover everything listed for it, either in its steps or in its More sections. Page ids (in brackets) are the names used in links and SHALL NOT change once published.

**Start here**
- **Build your first thing** (`quick-start`): open the Sample starter or start empty; add a beam from the library; turn, slide and zoom the view; add a pin; join the pin to the beam (Auto Join); add a second beam and join it; undo and redo; the build saves itself; where to go next (three links). Nothing else.
- **A tour of the screen** (`tour`): one picture of the whole app with a marker on each area and one line each: toolbar, build name and part count, Library, 3D view, context bar, view cube, Outline, Properties, the messages strip, the shortcuts strip; then the same for the tablet and the phone layout.

**Basics**
- **Finding and adding parts** (`library`): categories and their counts; search by name or part number; List and Grid; resting on a part for its name, size and big picture; Recent; click to add (where it lands); drag into the view to place it on a hole, stepping through fits; adding onto a marked hole; what to do by touch.
- **Looking around** (`looking-around`): turn, slide and zoom with mouse and with fingers; Focus (`F`); the view cube (faces, edges, corners, double-click); `1` `2` `3` and with Shift; where the cube's menu is.
- **Moving and turning parts** (`moving`): Move and Rotate (`W`, `E`, right-click the part); arrows and the flat squares; the Snap choices and what a pitch is; the part stopping when it touches another; rotate rings, picking a ring, the wheel, Turn step; World arrows; Free move; a part that overlaps turns red and goes back; typing a position or angle and the nudge and 90° buttons in Properties; colour.
- **Joining parts** (`joining`): lined-up holes light up through parts; Auto Join and Manual Join (double-click); mate arrows when there is more than one way; picking a hole and stepping through fits with the wheel bar; Mark a hole, then add from the library; a gear, hub or pulley going onto an axle end; sliding the battery into the Gen 1 brain; tight fits and overlaps; the messages that say what happened.
- **The context bar** (`context-bar`): where it is in each layout; every button, when it shows, what it does and its key; greyed buttons and their tips; buttons that stay on and ones armed for one use; the wheel bar with its tick and cross; pressing and holding for a tip.
- **Selecting and groups** (`selecting`): click; Shift + click and Add to selection; Select mode; box select both ways (`B`); Select all; Select Connected (`C`); growing and shrinking by joints; what a selection looks like; Group and Ungroup (`G`, `Shift+G`); One part of a group; moving, turning, hiding, locking, copying and deleting a set.
- **The Outline and Properties** (`outline`): the list of parts; its search; folders for groups; hide and lock; rename; Locate; the base name; checkboxes in Select mode; what Properties shows for one part, for a group and for several parts; how they open on a tablet and phone.
- **Copying** (`copying`): Duplicate (`D`); Stamp copies (Ctrl/Cmd + double-click a hole) and its fits; copying a group; a pointer to Mirror on the Advanced tools page.
- **Undo and the timeline** (`undo-history`): Undo and Redo (buttons and keys); what counts as a step and what doesn't; the History button and the timeline: marks, going to a step, Play and its speeds, a big step playing part by part and Go to end of step.
- **Saving and sharing** (`saving`): builds save themselves; the build's name and renaming it; File → New; every starter by name with a picture; Load… (Open, Rename, Duplicate, Delete, Open from computer…); Export and why to do it; Delete this build…; builds live only in this browser.

**Make it work like the real robot**
- **Pins, standoffs and connectors** (`pins-and-connectors`): which pin for how many layers; standoffs; the two corner connectors and which way each faces; checking a part that fits more than one way.
- **Shafts, wheels and motors** (`shafts-and-wheels`): plain shafts and motor shafts with the stop ring and where it goes; a free axle; spacers; pushing a wheel to the plate; tires on hubs (their overlap is meant); omni wheels and their lock beam; rubber collars go on last; mounting a Smart Motor on four pins; the two brains and the battery.
- **Gears** (`gears`): gears turn to mesh when placed; which sizes sit how far apart; square shafts in square holes; parts on one shaft turn together; the motor's hub; what "Meshed" messages mean; a pointer to the gear train builder on the Advanced tools page.
- **Chain and track** (`chain-and-track`): sprockets only, in one plane; select the sprockets, Tools → Chain builder; standoffs as guides; over or under questions; a part in the way; sag and how to get a snug fit; Track builder and its attachments and spacing; the chain is one group; removing it.
- **Rubber bands and belts** (`rubber-bands`): the band sizes; Tools → Band builder; picking spots in order (posts, pins, pulleys, gear gaps, beam edges); the other way round; Choose for me and stretch colours; Band between these / Belt round these; changing a band in Properties; bands follow their parts and pull in Play mode; why a band is refused.
- **Plastic sheets** (`plastic-sheets`): pin one or two rows flat first; select the sheet and the part to curve onto; Tools → Bend sheet…; choosing the rows; anchoring more rows; Flatten; stacked sheets; sheets flexing counts as a tight fit.

**See it and check it**
- **View options** (`views`): the cube's menu and the View button; Perspective and Orthographic (`P`); 4-View and its small views; Stereo, cross-eyed, Depth and Full screen; Grid; Wide / Panels.
- **Explode** (`explode`): Tools → Explode or `X`; the ring while it works; the slider and Play; the direction choices; what moves as one piece; big builds spread out; nothing is changed or saved by exploding.
- **Check and fix your build** (`check-and-fix`): Tools → Audit; the header and counters; every kind of finding in plain words (overlaps, loose pins, half-seated pins, unpinned parts, parts with too few pins, pieces not joined, shafts sticking out, notes and tight fits) and how to fix each; selecting a finding; Attempt Fix; Tools → Fix assembly on its own: what it lines up, which side moves, and what it says when it can't.
- **Blueprint** (`blueprint`): Tools → Blueprint; the three views; Open Full SVG; printing it from the browser.
- **Play mode** (`play-mode`): Tools → Play mode; the motor list and throttles; Stop; what turns and what holds still; Simulate gravity and the floor; Limit speed by load; parts stopping when they hit; bands pulling; the Drop test, its heights and what its result means; leaving Play mode puts everything back.

**Settings and devices**
- **Settings** (`settings`): the gear; Shadows; Ambient occlusion; Environment reflections and brightness; Advanced options and exactly what it adds; what is remembered in this browser.
- **Tablets and phones** (`tablets-and-phones`): the three layouts and when each is used; the icon toolbar; Library and Outline tabs and drawers and their pins; one finger, two fingers, tap; the context bar standing in for keys, right-click, double-click and the wheel; taps light a hole before placing; what can't be done by touch yet and the way round each.
- **Use it without internet** (`offline`): Settings → Offline → Use offline; the download, Cancel and Resume; Available offline; installing on Chrome or Edge, iPad and iPhone, and Safari on a Mac; the iPad order (Home Screen first); new versions; turning it off; Export as the backup.

**Look it up**
- **All shortcuts** (`shortcuts`): every key and mouse gesture in one table grouped by job, each with the context bar button or tap that does the same.
- **Words** (`words`): one line each for every special word the guide uses (pitch, pin, standoff, shaft, axle, bore, collar, spacer, hub, sprocket, mesh, mate, tight fit, overlap, group, context bar, view cube, Outline, Properties, starter and the rest), each linking to its page.
- **Something's not working** (`troubleshooting`): at least 15 short question and answer pairs for the things builders get stuck on (a part is red; a part won't join; nothing lights up; the wheel zooms when I want fits; I can't see a button the guide names; my build is gone; the app is slow; Explode leaves parts in place; a gear won't turn in Play mode; the chain sags; a band is refused; and the rest), each ending in a link to the page that covers it.

**Advanced** (each page starts by saying to turn on Settings → Advanced options)
- **Advanced tools** (`advanced-tools`): Properties' Smart Assembly section: Auto-Fasten Joint, Snap & Mate to Port (flip, roll, offset), the gear train builder and its presets, Mirror (left ↔ right, front ↔ rear, what to check after); the CAD information section; the Axes overlay.
- **History branches** (`history-branches`): Linear and Keep branches; branch markers on the timeline and switching branch.
- **The assistant** (`assistant`; dev app only): what the MCP badge means; the Assistant switch in Settings and what turning it off stops; the timeline's colours by who made a step.

#### Scenario: Every page exists
- **WHEN** the guide's page list is compared with this requirement
- **THEN** every page id above exists once, in its group and in this order, and there are no other pages

#### Scenario: A page covers its list
- **WHEN** a reviewer reads the Chain and track page with every More section open
- **THEN** each item listed for `chain-and-track` above is explained on it

#### Scenario: Quick start stays small
- **WHEN** the quick start page is read
- **THEN** it has at most 8 steps and 250 words, one picture per step, and no More sections other than "Where next"

### Requirement: Pages and sections carry tags
Pages SHALL carry two kinds of hidden labels, neither shown to the reader:
- **Topic tags** (`tags`): 3 to 10 words per page saying what the page is about (for example `gear`, `touch`, `mirror`, `zoom`). Many pages MAY share a topic tag. Topic tags SHALL list up to three related pages at the end of each page (the pages sharing the most topic tags) and SHALL rank search results that match a tag's words.
- **Situation claims** (`answers`): the situations this page, or one of its More sections, is the best answer for. A situation is one of the things Help can open on (see help-guide, "Help opens on what you're doing"): each tool, panel, view layout, activity in the 3D view, armed context bar button, Rotate mode, kind of part, and the group and several-parts selections. Each situation SHALL have a short reason used in the "Because you're …" line, SHALL be claimed by exactly one page, and MAY be claimed by one More section of any page, which then wins over the page. A kind of part no page explains (for example a sensor) SHALL NOT be a situation, so selecting one opens Help as if nothing were selected.

#### Scenario: Every situation has a page
- **WHEN** the help check runs
- **THEN** it fails if a situation has no page claiming it, or two pages or two sections claim the same situation, and it names the situation

#### Scenario: Unknown label
- **WHEN** a page carries a topic tag or a claim that isn't on the guide's lists
- **THEN** the help check fails and names the page and the label

#### Scenario: Enough topic tags
- **WHEN** a page carries fewer than 3 or more than 10 topic tags
- **THEN** the help check fails and names the page

#### Scenario: Related pages
- **WHEN** the reader reaches the end of the Chain and track page
- **THEN** up to three related pages are listed there, such as Gears and Rubber bands, and never the page itself

### Requirement: Every feature has a place in the guide
The project SHALL keep a coverage list: every user-facing feature of the app (each toolbar button and menu item, each context bar button, each panel and dialog, each setting, each keyboard shortcut and mouse or touch gesture, each starter, each tool in the Tools menu and each of its options) with the help page and section that explains it. Every entry SHALL point at a section that exists, and that section SHALL name the feature by the words the app shows for it. Where the app keeps a list of such things in one place in its code (the context bar's buttons, the starters), the check SHALL read that list, so a button or starter added to the app without a help entry fails the check.

#### Scenario: Nothing left out
- **WHEN** the help check runs
- **THEN** it passes only if every coverage entry points at an existing page section whose text contains the feature's on-screen name

#### Scenario: A new context bar button
- **WHEN** a button is added to the context bar's list and no help page names it
- **THEN** the help check fails and names the button

#### Scenario: A reader looks something up
- **WHEN** a reader wants to know what any button, menu item or setting in the app does
- **THEN** searching the guide for the words on that control finds the section that explains it

### Requirement: Written for middle schoolers
Help text SHALL follow these rules:
- Plain words, said to the reader ("Tap a hole", "You can…"). Short sentences: no sentence longer than 25 words, and a page's sentences average 15 words or fewer.
- A page's opening sentence is 20 words or fewer. Its steps: 6 or fewer for the main job, each 25 words or fewer and one action. Its top part (opening sentence and steps) is 120 words or fewer. A More section is 200 words or fewer; a longer one is split.
- Controls are named exactly as the app shows them, in bold, with the way to reach them written as **Tools → Explode**. Keys are shown as key caps.
- The same thing always has the same name: "3D view", "Library", "Outline", "Properties", "context bar", "view cube", "pitch", "shaft" (with "axle" given once as the same thing).
- No developer words: no file names, function names, code, part ids, "instance", "port", "BVH", "mesh" for a 3D model, "viewport", "kinematic", "semantic", "MCP" (outside the assistant page). Any other special word is in the Words page.
- Numbers only when the reader needs them (a pitch is 12.7 mm, or half an inch), and no tolerances outside More sections.
- Each More section's title says what is inside it ("Every button on the bar", "If the chain sags"), never just "Details".
- Tips and warnings are one sentence each, marked **Tip** or **Watch out**, and no page has more than two in its top part.

#### Scenario: Length limits
- **WHEN** the help check runs on a page whose top part has 140 words
- **THEN** it fails and names the page and the limit

#### Scenario: Developer words
- **WHEN** a page other than the assistant page contains "MCP" or a file name such as `App.jsx`
- **THEN** the help check fails and names the word and the page

#### Scenario: Names match the app
- **WHEN** a step tells the reader to choose a menu item
- **THEN** the words in bold are exactly the words on that menu item in the app

### Requirement: What the guide says is true
Every step in the guide SHALL have been done in the app as written, in the static site, with the mouse wording in the large layout and the touch wording in the medium or small layout, and SHALL produce what the page says. Text SHALL describe the app as it is, including what a feature can't do yet. A page SHALL NOT promise anything the app doesn't do.

#### Scenario: Following the quick start
- **WHEN** a person who has never used the app opens the static site in a fresh browser and follows the quick start in the large layout, and again by touch in the medium layout
- **THEN** every step works as written, and they finish with two beams joined by a pin in under five minutes

#### Scenario: Touch gaps are said
- **WHEN** the Finding and adding parts page is read with the touch wording
- **THEN** it says a part can't be dragged from the library by touch yet, and gives the way that works (mark a hole, then tap the part)

### Requirement: Pictures are taken from the app by a script
Every picture in the guide SHALL be made by one command that opens the static site in a fresh browser, puts the app in the state the picture needs, and saves the picture with the places of its markers. Running the command again SHALL remake every picture, or only the named ones. Pictures SHALL:
- show the real app with nothing edited in, from a starter or a small saved example build that is kept in the project;
- show only what the step is about (cropped to the controls and parts that matter, with some room round them), except the tour's full-screen pictures;
- use the large layout unless the step is about tablets, phones or touch, which use the medium or small layout;
- be sharp on high-density screens, no more than 150 KB each, and no more than 8 MB together;
- show no names, files or builds from the computer they were made on.

Every page SHALL have at least one picture in its top part, except All shortcuts and Words. Every step that names a control the reader hasn't used earlier on that page SHALL have a picture with a marker on it.

#### Scenario: Retaking one picture
- **WHEN** the Explode panel's layout changes and the command is run for the Explode page's pictures
- **THEN** those pictures and their markers are replaced and no other picture changes

#### Scenario: Markers follow the control
- **WHEN** the Tools button moves 40 px in the toolbar and the pictures are retaken
- **THEN** the marker in each retaken picture is on the Tools button without anyone editing a number

#### Scenario: Size limits
- **WHEN** the help check runs with a 300 KB picture in the guide
- **THEN** it fails and names the picture

#### Scenario: No stray pictures
- **WHEN** a picture file is in the guide's folder but no page uses it, or a page names a picture that isn't there
- **THEN** the help check fails and names it

### Requirement: The guide is checked and kept current
The project SHALL have a help check (one of the `scripts/check-*.mjs` scripts, exiting 1 on failure) that fails when: a page id is missing, extra or repeated; a link, picture, marker number or Show me control named on a page doesn't exist; a picture is unused, has no description or breaks the size limits; a page breaks the length or word rules; a coverage entry has no section; or the assistant page is not marked as dev-only. The project's notes for people and agents working on the app SHALL say that a change to what the app does updates the matching help page, its coverage entry and its pictures in the same piece of work.

#### Scenario: A broken link
- **WHEN** a page links to `help:gears#no-such-section`
- **THEN** the help check fails and names the page and the link

#### Scenario: A marker with no step
- **WHEN** a step says "(3)" and its picture has only two markers
- **THEN** the help check fails and names the page and the picture

#### Scenario: Clean run
- **WHEN** all pages, pictures and the coverage list are complete and within the rules
- **THEN** the help check exits 0
